# Stellar Buffs (Radiance: Stellar-Conduct System)

## 1. System Overview & Core Reaction
- **System Version**: Version 6.8 (Luna VIII Update)
- **Core Reaction**: **Stellar-Conduct** (Reworked Superconduct)
- **Reaction Enablers**: Sandrone, Cryo Traveler, Odette
- **Trigger**: Apply **Cryo** and **Electro** to an opponent while a qualifying Stellar Glimmer enabler is in the party.
- **Reaction Mechanics**:
  - Replaces traditional flat Superconduct reaction damage with high-scaling **ATK + Elemental Mastery** formula.
  - **Can score CRIT Hits** (scales with character Crit Rate and Crit DMG).
  - Applies **40% Physical RES Shred** and **15% Cryo & Electro RES Shred**.

---

## 2. Polestar Field & Radiance Mechanics
- **Polestar Field (Starlight Prism Arena)**:
  - Spawns upon triggering Stellar-Conduct and lasts **6 seconds** (refreshed on each subsequent reaction trigger).
  - Grants party-wide **+20% Cryo DMG Bonus** and **+20% Electro DMG Bonus** to all allies inside.
  - **Stacking Mechanic**: Accumulates **Polestar Stacks** (up to 10 stacks) every time Cryo or Electro damage hits inside the field.
  - **Starlight Nova**: Every 4s, the field releases a Starlight Nova shockwave that deals damage proportional to accumulated stacks.
- **Radiance: Stellar-Conduct**:
  - When designated characters fight inside the Polestar Field, they enter the **Radiance** state, unlocking transformed kit mechanics and numerical scaling.
